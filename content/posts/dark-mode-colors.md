title: Choosing Calm Colours for a Dark Theme
date: 2026-10-27
tags: [design, css]
summary: Why pure black is too harsh, and how to pick softer tones.
---

Pure black backgrounds with pure white text look sharp, but they tire your eyes quickly.

## Soften the background

Instead of `#000000`, I use a very dark green-grey. It's still dark, but it feels warmer.

## Soften the text

Instead of pure white, the main text is a light grey-green. Secondary text, like dates, is dimmer still.

## One accent colour

A single calm green for links and highlights is enough. Too many accent colours and nothing stands out.
