TypeScript sounds scary, but the core idea is simple: you put labels on your values.

## A label on a variable

```ts
const name: string = 'Leaf'
```

The `: string` part says "this is text". If you try to put a number there, your editor warns you before anything breaks.

## Why bother?

Because mistakes show up while you type, not later when a visitor finds them.
